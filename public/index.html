Before you begin

A few things to know.

Can I choose the Pokémon for a shundo hunt?

The offered shundo hunt delivers a random shundo. Discuss any specific request before ordering; do not assume a particular Pokémon is included.

What is included in 1K Random Catch?

The package includes 1,000 random Pokémon catches. Species are random. Confirm pricing, delivery time, available Poké Balls, and Pokémon storage before starting.

Are raid passes included?

No. You need to provide the required passes or arrange to buy them from the seller separately. Pass costs are separate from the 10-raid service.

How long does a service take?

Delivery time is confirmed for your request. Multiple shundos need more time. The stated Stardust farming rate is 1 million per 5 hours; discuss larger goals and scheduling before ordering.

What should I confirm before farming starts?

Confirm item usage and available storage. Farming uses your Poké Balls and Berries, and storage management may require discarding items. Agree on protected items and handling instructions before beginning.

How do I place an order?

Use the inquiry builder to prepare your request. Send it through your existing conversation with the seller and confirm pricing and terms. This website currently has no checkout or direct ordering connection.
